About

Product thinking, grounded in operations

UniLiv runs student and co-living properties across several Indian cities. Every property depends on dozens of daily routines: meals leaving a central kitchen on time, rooms cleaned and checked, geysers and pumps serviced before they fail. Most of that ran on WhatsApp groups and spreadsheets.

At UniLiv, the same failures kept showing up in the numbers: food wasted in the kitchen, geysers breaking before anyone serviced them, and audits that found problems nobody fixed. Reporting them wasn't enough, so I set out to fix them. Today I own the operations modules end to end. I spend time on site with Unit Leads, kitchen and housekeeping staff, turn what I see into PRDs, prioritise with engineering, run UAT and rollout, and keep tracking adoption until the numbers move.

My edge is that I don't need anyone to pull the data for me. I write the SQL, build the dashboard and decide what to change next.

How I work

From the property floor to the release note

The same loop runs through every case study. Discovery happens on site, not in a meeting room.

Shadow

Spend a shift with the kitchen team or Unit Lead and log every workaround they use.

Size it

Pull the data first. How often does it break, where, and what does it cost?

Decide

Lay out the options, score them with RICE and write down what we are not building.

Ship small

Pilot at 2–3 properties, run UAT with the real users, then roll out city by city.

Measure

Agree one north-star metric before launch and review it weekly for the first 90 days.
